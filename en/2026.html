The story

How have people used writing to preserve knowledge, and how do we keep handwriting alive in a digital age?

LETTERS · PENS · INK · PAPER · PEOPLE · WRITING CULTURE

Văn Miếu, Quốc Tử Giám, the Temple of Literature and Imperial Academy, is Vietnam’s symbol of learning and letters. In 1076 the Lý dynasty founded the Imperial Academy, the country’s first national university. Nearly a millennium later, its maxim “Talented scholars are the lifeblood of the nation” still speaks to every generation of learners. For the 950th anniversary, the Văn Miếu, Quốc Tử Giám Centre for Cultural and Scientific Activities and Vietnam Pen Show turn Hồ Văn into a meeting place for writers, collectors, artisans, brands and the international pen community.
