Our mission

Vietnam Pen brings together people who love pens, love letters and value the culture of handwriting

Together we explore, preserve and share what pens, ink, paper, art and the history of writing have to offer. From Vietnam, we want to bring the stories of Vietnamese writing culture closer to the rest of the world.

Every Pen Show

What you will find again each year

I

Pen museum

Pens that carry the memory of their era, from Japan, Europe and America, China and Vietnam.

II

Brand booths

Meet Vietnamese and international pen brands, and try pens and inks on the spot.

III

Pen Clinic

Nib tuning, repairs and servicing for your own pen, alongside the craftspeople.

IV

Ink mixing and personalisation

Mix your own ink colour, then assemble and personalise a pen of your own.

V

Writing workshops

Calligraphy, Vietnamese calligraphy, Hán Nôm script, sketching and journaling.

VI

Talks and meetups

Collectors’ stories, the International Pen Talk and the Pen Community Meet.
